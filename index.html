I

Chapter one · Sri Lanka · Days 1–10 · 31 December – 9 January

The Southern Coast

Colombo, Galle, Unawatuna, Mirissa · sea level

Shake off the winter. Take the coastal train from Colombo to Galle, spend four days learning to dive in Unawatuna, and go out one morning from Mirissa to look for blue whales.

Practical

Transport
Sit on the right of the train for the sea.
PADI course
Do the dive theory online before you fly.
Visa
Sri Lanka's visa is currently free for Germans, but you still need the online ETA.
Historical Galle Fort ramparts at sunset Experience PADI Open Water in Unawatuna, 3–4 days Wildlife Blue whales off Mirissa, in season until April
II

Chapter two · Sri Lanka · Days 11–19 · 10–18 January

The Hill Country

Ella, Adam's Peak, Kandy, Sigiriya · up to 2,243 m

The slow blue train climbs into tea country and you need a sweater for the first time in weeks. Ella for walking, then a night on Adam's Peak: off the train at Hatton, a few hours' sleep in Dalhousie, and 5,200 lit steps with the pilgrims to watch the sunrise from Sri Pada, the summit with the sacred footprint. Then Kandy for the Temple of the Tooth and Sigiriya for the fortress on its rock.

Checked in October 2026Cyclone Ditwah damaged the rail network. The hill line has run again since June 2026; Colombo to Kandy was due back in October. Recheck in December and take a car if needed.

Practical

Train tickets
Reserved seats sell out; book 30 days ahead or ride unreserved.
Adam's Peak
Climb the night of Tue 12 to Wed 13 January: in pilgrimage season (December to May), and clear of the weekend crowds of up to 20,000 people and of the Duruthu full moon around 22 January. Leave Dalhousie around 2:30 for sunrise near 6:30; 2.5–4 hours up.
Clothing
Bring a fleece and a warm layer for the summit; it is cold and windy at dawn.
Experience Ella to Kandy by train, doors open Pilgrimage Sunrise from Adam's Peak, and its shadow on the clouds Historical Evening puja at the Temple of the Tooth Historical Sigiriya at 06:30, before the heat and the buses
III

Chapter three · South India · Days 20–26 · 19–25 January

The Spice Coast

Kochi, Munnar · up to 1,600 m

A short flight and India arrives all at once. Start gently in Fort Kochi, with its Portuguese churches, Dutch houses and Chinese fishing nets, then climb into the cardamom and tea hills of Munnar.

Practical

Getting to Munnar
Bus Kochi to Munnar takes about four hours of hairpins.
Season
Late January is peak season in Kerala; book rooms a few days ahead.
Cultural The fishing nets lowered at dusk Cultural Kathakali; come early for the make-up Landscape Walks through Munnar's tea estates
IV

Chapter four · South India · Days 27–57 · 26 January – 25 February

The Ashram

Sivananda ashram, Neyyar Dam · 100 m

A month at the Sivananda Yoga Vedanta Dhanwantari Ashram. Bell at 05:20, meditation and chanting, two asana classes a day, karma yoga, Vedanta lectures. No meat, no alcohol, no leaving the grounds.

Open decisionStill to decide: yoga vacation or teacher training. With a 31 December start, the January course falls during Sri Lanka; the next one begins 14 February.

Practical

Which programme
The year-round yoga vacation fits these dates (26 Jan–25 Feb), at roughly $20 a night each; check Neyyar's current rate. The teacher training runs 14 Feb–13 Mar for $1,466–2,635 each and would mean doing Goa and Hampi before the ashram.
Rooms
Ask whether a couple may share a room; other Sivananda ashrams don't allow it.
Dress code
White or yellow clothes, shoulders and knees covered.
Experience Morning satsang as light reaches the reservoir Experience Week three, when the poses stop hurting Cultural An hour of karma yoga each day

Chapter five · South India · Days 58–64 · 26 February – 4 March

The Backwaters

Varkala, Alleppey · sea level

Out of the ashram gates and straight to Varkala's red cliffs over the Arabian Sea, then one night on a private houseboat in Alleppey with the canals sliding past. A slow way back into the world.

Practical

Houseboats
Book the houseboat in person in Alleppey so you can see the boat first.
Swimming
The currents under the cliffs are strong.
Experience Overnight on a kettuvallam houseboat Landscape Varkala's cliff path at golden hour Cultural An Ayurvedic massage for the ashram stiffness

VII

Chapter seven · Central India · Days 73–86 · 13–26 March

Goa, the slow shore

Palolem, Agonda · sea level

Two weeks of doing nothing on purpose before North India. Skip the party beaches of the north, find a hut in Palolem or Agonda by walking the sand, and rent a scooter.

Practical

Scooter hire
Scooters cost about €4 a day; carry an International Driving Permit.
Huts
Beach huts are rebuilt each season, so don't prebook.
Onward
Fly Goa to Varanasi.
Cultural A swim before breakfast Cultural Scooter rides through paddy fields to empty coves Culinary Fish curry soured with kokum
VIII

Chapter eight · North India · Days 87–104 · 27 March – 13 April

The Holy Cities

Varanasi, Rishikesh, Amritsar · 80–370 m

India at full volume. The burning ghats of Varanasi, the cold green Ganges at Rishikesh, and the Golden Temple at Amritsar, whose free kitchen feeds tens of thousands every day.

Practical

Trains
Train bookings open 60 days ahead. The first 15 minutes are reserved for Aadhaar holders, so book after that or use the foreign tourist quota.
Respect
Never photograph cremations. Cover your head at the Golden Temple.
Cultural A rowing boat past Manikarnika ghat at dawn Experience Swimming or rafting the upper Ganges Historical An hour peeling garlic in the Golden Temple kitchen
IX

Chapter nine · Split paths · Days 105–125 · 14 April – 4 May

Diverging Paths

Tapovan for Micky, the Annapurna Circuit for Mo · up to 5,416 m

Three weeks apart. Micky stays in Tapovan above Rishikesh for a breathwork training. Mo flies to Kathmandu, takes the bus to Besisahar and a jeep to Chame, and walks the Annapurna Circuit over the Thorong La to Jomsom and Pokhara.

Open decisionIndia allows 90 days per visit on an e-visa. Micky would be in India about 105 days, so she has to leave once before 18 April (day 109), for example a long weekend in Nepal, on a multiple-entry e-visa.

Practical

Guide & permits
A licensed guide has officially been required in the Annapurna area since 2023. Book one through an agency, which also sorts the permits.
Thorong La
Mid-April is peak season, so book early. The pass is still far below freezing before dawn, and new snow can close it for a day or two. Keep two buffer days.
Nepal visa
Mo: 30-day Nepal visa on arrival, $50.
Experience Breathwork in Tapovan Landscape Teahouse to teahouse round the Annapurna massif Milestone Thorong La, 5,416 m, by headlamp at 4 a.m.

Chapter ten · Nepal · Days 126–132 · 5–11 May

Kathmandu, the threshold

Patan, Boudhanath, Nagarkot · 1,400 m

Back together, and a calm week before China. Rooftop tea in Patan, the walk around Boudhanath at dusk, one night at Nagarkot for the whole Himalayan crest. Buy down jackets.

Practical

Where to stay
Stay in Patan or Boudha rather than Thamel.
Air quality
Bring a mask; the valley air is poor in spring.
Cultural The kora around Boudhanath as the lamps are lit Historical Patan Durbar Square Landscape Sunrise over the Himalaya from Nagarkot
XI

Chapter eleven · Tibet · Days 133–148 · 12–27 May

Lhasa and Everest

Chengdu, Lhasa, Gyantse, Shigatse, Everest · 3,656 m, up to 5,000 m

The road from Kathmandu is closed, so you come in from the other side: fly to Chengdu and on to Lhasa. Four days acclimatising around the Jokhang and the Barkhor, then a six-day loop by car through Yamdrok, Gyantse and Shigatse to Rongbuk under Everest's north face, and back to Lhasa.

Practical

Tour & permit
A guide and a booked tour are required the whole time. The agency needs your passports at least 20 days ahead and delivers the permit to your Chengdu hotel.
Closures
Tibet usually closes to foreigners in February and March. By mid-May it should be open again, but confirm before paying.
Altitude
Pack Diamox; get it from your Hausarzt.

If plans change

Plan A, now the plan
Fly Kathmandu to Chengdu on your own L visa and join the Tibet tour there. You stay free to travel on to Xinjiang alone.
Plan B
If the Kyirong border reopens and China extends visa-free entry into 2027, take the original eight days overland from Kathmandu. Avoid the group visa sold in Kathmandu: it cancels your L visa and ties you to the group's exit.
Plan C
If Tibet is closed, go overland from Chengdu through western Sichuan (Kangding, Tagong, Litang) or visit Rebkong and Labrang near Xining, then rejoin the route at Xining.
Cultural The Jokhang at dawn with the pilgrims Cultural Monks debating in the courtyard at Sera Landscape First light on Everest from Rongbuk

Chapter twelve · China · Days 149–150 · 28–29 May

The Sky Train

Lhasa to Xining · up to 5,072 m

Twenty-one hours on the highest railway in the world, with oxygen at every berth. Over the Tanggula Pass in the afternoon, across Hoh Xil at dusk, into Xining at dawn.

Practical

The right train
Z8982 leaves at 09:50 and arrives at 06:21 (October 2026 timetable; it may be seasonal, so recheck in April).
Tickets
Tickets go on sale 15 days ahead on Trip.com or 12306. Take soft sleeper: four berths and a door, about ¥780 each.
Landscape Tanggula Pass, around 16:00 Landscape Antelope and wild ass on the Hoh Xil plain Landscape The Qiangtang grasslands from the corridor window

Chapter thirteen · China · Days 151–162 · 30 May – 10 June

The Hexi Corridor

Xining, Zhangye, Jiayuguan · 1,500–2,300 m

The Silk Road's narrow passage between the Tibetan plateau and the Gobi, now at 250 km/h. Kumbum monastery near Xining, the striped Danxia hills at Zhangye, the western end of the Ming wall at Jiayuguan.

Practical

Trains
High-speed rail throughout; Xining to Zhangye is about 2.5 hours.
Language
English is rare. Keep hotel addresses in Chinese and an offline translator.

Chapter fourteen · China · Days 163–167 · 11–15 June

Dunhuang, where the desert meets the Buddha

Mogao caves, Crescent Lake · 1,140 m

An oasis at the edge of the Gobi. The Mogao caves hold a thousand years of Buddhist painting; outside town a crescent-shaped spring sits between hundred-metre dunes.

Practical

Mogao tickets
Book Mogao about 30 days ahead. The official site often needs a Chinese phone number, so use Trip.com or an agency.
Heat & sand
Mid-June days pass 30 °C. Climb the dunes at sunrise or after 18:00, with a buff for the sand.
Cultural Mogao caves on the English tour Landscape Mingsha dunes and Crescent Lake at sunset Historical Yumen Pass, where Han China ended
XV

Chapter fifteen · China · Days 168–176 · 16–24 June

Turpan, the oasis of fire

Turpan, Ürümqi · −154 m

Below sea level, ringed by the Flaming Mountains, and green with vines fed by hand-dug karez channels. This is where the trip turns Uyghur: lamb, nan, laghman, the call to prayer.

Practical

Heat
June highs average about 37 °C and often pass 40 °C. Go out early and late.
Xinjiang rules
Foreigners may only stay in licensed hotels. Carry your passport for frequent checkpoints, and don't fly drones.
Time zones
Xinjiang time runs two hours behind Beijing time. Check which one a ticket uses.
Historical Jiaohe ruins at sunrise Landscape Grape Valley under the trellises Culinary A walk down into a karez

Chapter sixteen · China · Days 177–183 · 25 June – 1 July

Kashgar, the last city of China

Kashgar, Karakul Lake · 1,290 m, Karakul 3,600 m

The old crossroads of the southern Silk Road. Samsa from the tonur ovens of the Old Town, the Sunday livestock bazaar on 27 June, then a night at Karakul Lake under Muztagh Ata.

Practical

Border permit
The Karakoram Highway needs a border permit. It's free and takes about 30 minutes at the Kashgar airport desk; get it even if Karakul alone may not need it.
Karakul
A private driver to Karakul and back costs about €80.
Cultural The Sunday livestock market, busiest from 10 to 11 Cultural Id Kah mosque on Friday afternoon Landscape A yurt night at Karakul

Chapter seventeen · Border · Days 184–186 · 2–4 July

Across the Irkeshtam

Kashgar to Sary-Tash to Osh · Sary-Tash 3,170 m

The quiet back door out of China. The exit stamp at Ulugqat, a long no-man's-land, then the Alay valley opens with the Pamirs along one side. A cold night in Sary-Tash, then down to Osh.

Practical

Border hours
Open Monday to Friday only, closed roughly 13:00–16:00 and on Chinese holidays. You reach it on Friday 2 July, so cross that morning or a day early. Any delay means waiting until Monday.
Logistics
Arrange the Chinese side through an agency; shared taxis alone are unreliable for foreigners.
Kyrgyz visa
Kyrgyzstan: 60 days visa-free.
Landscape The Alay valley Historical Sulaiman-Too above Osh at sunset Cultural Jayma bazaar in Osh

Epilogue · A Practical Reference

Everything you'll actually need to know.

What changed after the October 2026 check, what the trip really costs, and what to arrange before you leave. Day 1 is 31 December, the flight to Colombo.

Checked in October 2026

  • Nepal to Tibet by road. Both crossings listed as closed indefinitely (25 Sep 2026). Fly Kathmandu to Chengdu and enter Tibet from China (Plan A).
  • China visa. Visa-free entry ends 31 Dec 2026 and allows 30 days. You'll be in China about 52 days from mid-May. Apply in Germany for a multiple-entry L visa, 60 days per stay, valid at least until July. A single-entry visa expires three months after issue, before you reach China.
  • India's 90-day rule. Micky would stay about 105 days in a row. One exit before 18 April on a multiple-entry e-visa.
  • Ashram programme. The January teacher training clashes with a 31 December start. Take the year-round yoga vacation as planned, or the 14 Feb course with Goa and Hampi moved first.
  • Annapurna guide rule. Licensed guide officially required since 2023. Book guide and permits through an agency.
  • Summer heat. Turpan in late June and Bukhara in late July often pass 40 °C. Sightsee early and late; rest at midday.
  • Irkeshtam pass. Weekdays only, long lunch closure. You arrive on Friday 2 July. Cross that morning or on Thursday.
  • Sri Lankan trains. Cyclone repairs still underway in places. Recheck in December.
  • Georgia entry. Insurance is mandatory since January 2026. Carry proof of cover, GEL 30,000 minimum.
  • Fine as planned. The sky train runs, and Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and Georgia are visa-free for 60, 30 and 365 days.

Before You Go

  • Now: Decide on the ashram programme: the year-round yoga vacation from 26 January, or the teacher training from 14 February. Ask whether you can share a room.
  • November–December: China multiple-entry L visa, 60 days per stay, valid until at least July. One-year multiple-entry India e-visas. Sri Lanka ETA. Insurance with rescue cover to 5,500 m and a certificate for Georgia. Start rabies shots.
  • January: Book the Tibet tour, Mo's Annapurna guide and Micky's breathwork training. From 26 January, Indian trains for late March open.
  • April: Micky leaves India once before 18 April. Confirm Tibet permits and that the Z8982 is running.
  • May–June: Mogao about 30 days ahead, sky train tickets on 13 May, Afrosiyob tickets from late May, and an agency for the Chinese side of Irkeshtam on 2 July.

Money & Connectivity

  • South Asia: Cash rules. Carry some USD or EUR as backup.
  • China: Set up Alipay or WeChat Pay with a foreign card before arriving. Install a VPN before you cross, too.
  • Central Asia & Georgia: Cards in Tashkent and Tbilisi; som and lari for bazaars and marshrutkas.
  • Cards: Bring three from different banks. One will get swallowed or blocked.
  • Signal: Regional eSIMs skip the Indian SIM paperwork. Expect none around Everest, on the Annapurna and in the Pamirs.

Health & Packing

  • Vaccinations: Hepatitis A and B, typhoid, tetanus, and rabies for the dogs and remote stretches.
  • Water: Unsafe from the tap until Tbilisi. Carry a filter bottle.
  • Altitude: Diamox from your Hausarzt. At altitude, one beer counts as three.
  • Climate spectrum: From 38°C in Bukhara to well below freezing at Rongbuk. Down jacket, thermals and linen.
  • Modesty: Whites for the ashram; shoulders and knees covered for temples and mosques.
